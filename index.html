01 · Flagship project — Deq

The people you forget cost you time.money.deals.status.

Founders, recruiters and business people meet people constantly, then forget who they were, what was said, and to follow up. Deq fixes that.

Why I built it

The day I left India, a well-connected businessman told me to stay in touch. Months later I couldn't remember his name, his face or his number. Deq exists so that doesn't happen again.

02 · Project — VisionBrush

VisionBrush.
Draw with your eyes.

A drawing canvas for people with limited hand movement. Your gaze moves the brush; your voice starts and ends each stroke.

Webcam eye trackingVoice commandsRuns in the browserNothing recorded
How it works

You follow a few dots so it learns your eyes. Then say “start” and “end” to draw a stroke, and “fix” to snap shapes clean. No speech support? Hold your gaze to place points and close your eyes for a second to start or stop. Built as a three-person team for AME 434 at ASU.
